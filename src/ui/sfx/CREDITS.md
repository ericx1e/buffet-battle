# Sound credits

All sound effects here are by Kenney (www.kenney.nl), released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0): free to use,
attribution appreciated but not required.

- Impact Sounds: plates, glass, wood, soft impacts, punches, the bell
- RPG Audio: coins, the knife chop and slice, the metal pot, the fridge door, book page flips, cloth
- Interface Sounds: tick, pluck, drop, error, glass dings
- Music Jingles: the NES and steel-drum jingles (jLevel, jCook, jWin, jTrophy, jLose, jLife, jDraw)

They were converted from .ogg to short WAVs with `tools/import-sfx.mjs`. Burn's sizzle is synthesized in
`src/ui/sound.ts`.
